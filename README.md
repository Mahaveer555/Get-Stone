# GET STONE — Kota Stone, Popular Marble & Granite Supplier Website

**Digital showroom and quotation platform for GET STONE in Kota, Rajasthan, India.**

Includes complete natural stone collections:
- **Kota Stone & Limestones** (from `atishaystone.in`)
- **Popular Marble & Exotic Granites** (High-resolution photos & varieties from `rkmarble.com/popular-marble/` and `rkmarble.com/granite-by-colour/`)

## 🧭 Routes Available
- `/` — Homepage with Kota Stone + Popular Marble & Granite Showcase tabs
- `/products/popular-marble` — Dedicated Popular Marble page with 15 marble varieties and slab zoom lightbox
- `/products/granite` — Dedicated Exotic Granite page with 10 colour granites and slab zoom lightbox
- `/products/kota-stone` — Semi Quality, High Polish, Multi Colour, Diamond Cut Kota Stone
- `/products/river-wash-stone` — River Wash & Brown River Wash Kota Stone
- `/products/kota-tiles` — Calibrated Kota tiles and export packaging
- `/products/red-mandana-stone` — Red Mandana Stone
- `/products/kota-cobbles` — 4×4 Cobbles for outdoor paving
- `/about` — About Us page
- `/gallery` — Filterable Gallery (Kota Stone, Popular Marble, Exotic Granite)
- `/contact` — Contact, quotation form, and Google Maps
